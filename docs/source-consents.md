# Client Consent to be Recorded

Client Consent to be Recorded

Client consent to be recorded  
**Purpose of Recording:** Thank you for participating in our telehealth sessions. To enhance the quality of our services, your session will be recorded both visually and verbally through Zoom. These recordings will be used exclusively for the purpose of generating an accurate transcript via our session transcription tool. The transcript will be provided solely to your practitioner for review and reference and allows your practitioner to stay fully present for sessions without taking notes.

**Confidentiality & Security:** We prioritize your privacy and confidentiality. The recorded video and audio will not be used for any purpose beyond transcription and will never be shared, published, or distributed. All recordings and transcripts are securely stored and protected in accordance with industry standards to ensure your data remains private and confidential.

**Voluntary Consent:** Your participation in the recording is entirely voluntary. You have the right to decline or withdraw your consent at any time before or during the session. If you choose to withdraw consent, no further recordings will take place, and any existing recordings will be deleted as per our data retention policy.

**Acknowledgment & Agreement:** By typing your name on the next page, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this consent form and agree to be recorded during your session for the purpose of transcription. You understand that your information will remain confidential and secure, and that you may withdraw consent at any time.
